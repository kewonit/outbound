Privacy.

The app has no account and no analytics. The painting happens on your Mac. This policy says exactly what Outbound and this website do with personal data, who handles it, and what you can ask of us.

1. Who we are

  • Outbound is made and sold by edbnme ("we", "us"). We decide how the personal data described here is used, which makes us its controller under the GDPR and UK GDPR, the business under U.S. state privacy laws, and the data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, where those laws apply.
  • This policy covers the Outbound app, from this website or the Mac App Store, and the website at outbound.edbn.me.
  • Write to outbound@edbn.me about anything here. We haven't appointed a data protection officer, as we aren't required to; that address reaches the person who makes Outbound.

2. In short

  • The app collects nothing about you or how you use it. No analytics, ads, tracking or crash reports.
  • The copy from the Mac App Store makes no network requests at all.
  • The copy from this website goes online only to check its license and to look for updates.
  • This website counts visits with Plausible, which sets no cookies and keeps nothing that identifies you.
  • Payments are taken by Polar on this website, and by Apple on the App Store. We never see your card.
  • When you buy on this website, X Ads is told of the sale, so we can see which ads lead to one. See Ads on X.
  • We don't sell personal data, and we don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. The App Store copy

  • It makes no network requests. There is no key to check, and the App Store brings its updates.
  • Apple handles the purchase, the payment and any refund. We get sales figures from Apple, not who you are. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • It keeps its settings and paintings in its own sandbox, in ~/Library/Containers/app.outbound.Outbound.
  • Support, Feedback and Privacy in the app open this website in your browser, where section 6 applies.

4. The copy from this website

The license check

  • When you buy from the app, it asks this website to start a checkout, then fetches your key once you've paid. The website keeps nothing about you, and tells X Ads of the sale as described under Ads on X.
  • When you unlock Outbound, it sends your license key and your Mac's name to Polar, the store that sells it, so the key can count which Macs use it.
  • About every two weeks it asks Polar whether the key is still active. Offline is fine for a month.
  • Deactivating in Settings tells Polar to free that Mac's place.

Updates

  • Once a day Outbound asks this website whether there is a new version, and downloads it from GitHub if there is. Nothing about you is sent beyond what any web request carries: your IP address, and the versions of Outbound and macOS.

5. What stays on your Mac

  • Your license key, in your keychain (the copy from this website only).
  • Your settings, in the app's preferences.
  • Paintings of the current view, used as your desktop picture, and a record of the desktop pictures they cover, so yours can be put back.
  • Outbound reads where the cursor is so the bird can follow it. It never records it or sends it anywhere, and needs no special permission.
  • None of this leaves your Mac. Quit Outbound and drag it to the Trash to remove it; its files are in ~/Library/Application Support/Outbound, or in the sandbox above for the App Store copy.

6. This website

Hosting

  • The website runs on Cloudflare, which receives your IP address, browser and the page asked for in order to deliver and protect it. We don't keep logs of visits ourselves. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • It picks your language from your browser's settings, and your country from your IP address to show prices in your money. Neither is stored.

Counting visits

  • Visits are counted with Plausible: which pages are seen, where visitors came from, and their country, browser and kind of device. It sets no cookies and keeps nothing that identifies you. On the thank-you page it gets only the page, not the checkout in its address. See Plausible's data policy.
  • It also counts clicks on Buy and Download, which questions in the FAQ are opened, and finished purchases with their price before tax, never who made them.

What this website keeps in your browser

  • A lang cookie, for a year, only if you pick a language at the bottom of a page. It is sent back so the right language is shown, and does nothing else.
  • After you buy, your key, for a year, so the homepage can show it if you lose it. It never leaves your browser. Click Forget to remove it.
  • If you arrive from an ad on X, the click id X added to its link, for a month. It goes only to the checkout.
  • That you joined the waitlist and for which platform, and which order this browser has already counted, so neither is repeated. During a checkout, its id, in that tab only.
  • Clearing this website's data in your browser removes all of it.

7. Buying on this website

  • Outbound is sold through Polar, the merchant of record, which takes the payment, charges and pays the tax, sends your receipt and handles refunds. Polar collects your name, email address, billing country and payment details under Polar's privacy policy. We never see or store card details.
  • Polar gives us your email address, country, what you bought and what you paid, and creates your license key. We use them to deliver your key, to help when you write, and for our records.
  • If you buy your city painted in, we use your email address to agree which place to paint and to send it to you.

8. Ads on X

  • Outbound is advertised on X. To learn which ads lead to sales, this website tells X Ads about each paid order, once: when it was made, what it came to before tax, and its order number.
  • With it goes your email address, hashed (turned into a code X can match against its own accounts but can't turn back into the address). When the thank-you page fetches your key, your IP address and browser go too, and if you arrived from an ad on X, the click id X added to its link.
  • Free orders and App Store purchases aren't told. There is no X pixel or X cookie on this website. See X's privacy policy.
  • To object, email us before you buy and we'll leave your order out.

9. The waitlist

  • If you ask to hear when Outbound comes to Windows or iPhone, your email address is kept with the platform you picked, the page's language and the date. Nothing else is collected.
  • It is stored with Supabase, which hosts the database, and is used for one email when that version is out. Then it's deleted. It is never shared or used for anything else. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • To be taken off sooner, email outbound@edbn.me.

10. Feedback and email

  • What you write on the feedback page is kept with Supabase, with the page's language and the date. Opened from the app, it also notes the app's version, and how the painting runs on that Mac: its chip, macOS version and number of displays, what was holding the painting still, and how quickly the Mac's graphics paint it, with any error they gave.
  • Your email address is kept only if you give it, and only used to reply.
  • Email you send to outbound@edbn.me is forwarded by Cloudflare to our mailbox, and kept as long as it helps to answer you.
  • Feedback and email are read by the person who makes Outbound, and never shared. To have them deleted, ask.

11. Why we use it

  • To deliver what you bought, and to support it: your order, key, Macs and messages. This is needed to perform our contract with you.
  • To run and protect the website, count visits and learn which ads lead to sales, in ways that don't identify you to us. These are our legitimate interests, and you can object to them.
  • To email you about Windows or iPhone, or reply to feedback, because you asked us to. You can withdraw that at any time.
  • To keep records the law requires, such as tax records, which Polar and Apple keep as the sellers of record.
  • We make no automated decisions about you that have legal or similar effects, and build no profiles.

12. Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare: hosting, security and email forwarding for this website.
  • Polar: payments, tax, receipts, refunds and license keys for purchases on this website.
  • Apple: purchases, updates and refunds for the App Store copy.
  • Plausible: counting visits, without cookies or personal data.
  • Supabase: the database for the waitlist and feedback.
  • X: measuring which ads lead to sales, as described above.
  • GitHub: hosting the downloads and the source code. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • We share personal data only with these services, only for these purposes, and otherwise only when the law requires it or to protect our rights. If Outbound is ever sold, it would pass to the new owner under this policy.

13. International transfers

  • We are based in India, and the services above work in many countries, including the United States. Your personal data may be handled outside the country where you live.
  • Where the GDPR, UK GDPR or Swiss law requires it, those transfers rely on an adequacy decision, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where a service is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in the services' data processing terms.

14. How long we keep it

  • Orders, receipts and keys: as long as Polar or Apple must keep them for tax and accounting, usually several years.
  • The order numbers already told to X: 90 days, so none is told twice.
  • The waitlist: until the one email is sent, then deleted.
  • Feedback and email: as long as they help to answer you or improve Outbound, and deleted sooner when you ask.
  • Visit counts: Plausible keeps only totals, which identify no one.

15. Security

  • Everything travels over HTTPS. The website sends a strict Content Security Policy and other security headers, and its tokens for Polar, Supabase and X are kept as secrets that can do only what the website needs.
  • On your Mac, the license key is kept in your keychain.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever put your personal data at risk, we would tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
  • To report a security problem, email outbound@edbn.me.

16. Your rights

  • Depending on where you live, you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to receive it in a portable form, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent you gave. We won't treat you differently for asking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you can also complain to your data protection authority.
  • United States: we don't sell personal data. Telling X Ads about a purchase may count as sharing it for advertising under some state laws; email us to opt out.
  • India: under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask for a summary of your data and how it is used, have it corrected or erased, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance with us. If you aren't satisfied, you can approach the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Email outbound@edbn.me from the address you used, and we'll answer within a month, or sooner where the law requires. For purchases, Polar or Apple may need to act too, and we'll help.

17. Children

  • Outbound and this website aren't directed to children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA and UK, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you think a child has given us some, email us and we'll delete it.

18. Changes

  • If this policy changes, the new version will be here with a new date at the top. If a change matters, we'll say so on this page before it takes effect.
