Privacy.

Outbound has no servers and no account. Your prompts stay on your Mac.

In short

  • Outbound collects nothing about you.
  • It makes no network requests. It works offline.
  • No analytics, no ads, no crash reports, no built-in updater.

What Outbound stores

  • Your prompts and categories, in one file: ~/Library/Application Support/Outbound/Library.json.
  • Each prompt keeps its title, text, category, and the dates it was made and last changed.
  • Your shortcut, in the app preferences.
  • Open at login is kept by macOS, and is off until you turn it on.
  • If the file cannot be read, it is moved aside and kept. It is never deleted.

Permissions

  • Accessibility lets Outbound paste for you. It sends one ⌘V to the app in front, only after you pick a prompt.
  • It does not watch your typing, read other apps, or see your screen.
  • Without Accessibility, Outbound copies the prompt and you paste it yourself.
  • Files are read only when you choose them to import.

The clipboard

  • Using a prompt puts its text on the clipboard, so you can paste it again.
  • Outbound reads the clipboard only when you paste into it.
  • Clipboard managers you run may save what Outbound copies.

Your control

  • Back up or move your prompts by copying the file.
  • A deleted prompt leaves the file on the next save. ⌘Z brings it back while Outbound is open.
  • To remove everything, quit Outbound, delete the app, and delete the Outbound folder in Application Support.
