Privacy.

The app has no account and no analytics. The painting happens on your Mac.

In short

  • Outbound collects nothing about you or how you use it.
  • No analytics, ads or crash reports in the app.
  • The only network requests are for your license and for updates, described below.

The license check

  • When you buy from the app, it asks this website to start a checkout, then fetches your key once you've paid. The website keeps nothing.
  • When you unlock Outbound, it sends your license key and your Mac's name to Polar, the store that sells it, so the key can count which Macs use it.
  • About every two weeks it asks Polar whether the key is still active. Offline is fine for a month.
  • Deactivating in Settings tells Polar to free that Mac's place.
  • Payments are handled by Polar. See Polar's privacy policy.

Updates

  • Once a day Outbound asks this website whether there is a new version, and downloads it from GitHub if there is. Nothing about you is sent.

What Outbound stores on your Mac

  • Your license key, in your keychain.
  • Your settings, in the app preferences.
  • Paintings of the current view, in ~/Library/Application Support/Outbound/Stills, used as your desktop picture.
  • The desktop picture you had before, so it can be put back.

On this website

  • Visits are counted with Plausible: which pages are seen, where visitors came from, and their country, browser and kind of device. It sets no cookies and keeps nothing that identifies you. On the thank-you page it gets only the page, not the checkout in its address.
  • It also counts clicks on Buy and Download, which questions in the FAQ are opened, and finished purchases with their price before tax, never who made them.
  • After you buy, your key is saved in this browser for a year, so the homepage can show it if you lose it. It never leaves your browser. Click Forget to remove it.

The waitlist

  • If you ask to hear when Outbound comes to Windows or iPhone, your email address is kept with the platform you picked, the page's language and the date. Nothing else is collected.
  • It is stored with Supabase, which hosts the database, and is used for one email when that version is out. Then it's deleted. It is never shared or used for anything else.
  • To be taken off sooner, email outbound@edbn.me.
  • This browser also remembers that you signed up, and for which platform, so the page shows that instead of the form. Your address isn't kept there, and none of it leaves your browser.

Feedback

  • What you write on the feedback page is kept with Supabase, with the page's language and the date. Opened from the app, it also notes the app's version.
  • Your email is kept only if you give it, and only used to reply.
  • It is read by the person who made Outbound, and never shared. To have it deleted, email outbound@edbn.me.

Your cursor

  • Outbound reads where the cursor is so the bird can follow it. It never records it, and needs no special permission.

Questions
