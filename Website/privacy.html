Privacy.

No servers, no account. Your prompts stay on your Mac.

In short

  • Outbound collects nothing.
  • It makes no network requests and works offline.
  • No analytics, ads, crash reports, or updater.

What Outbound stores

  • Your prompts and categories, in ~/Library/Application Support/Outbound/Library.json.
  • For each prompt: its title, text, category, and when it was made and last changed.
  • Your shortcut, in the app preferences.
  • Open at login is a macOS setting. Change it in Settings.
  • If the file cannot be read, it is moved aside, never deleted.

Permissions

  • Accessibility lets Outbound paste. It sends one ⌘V to the front app, only after you pick a prompt.
  • It does not watch your typing, read other apps, or see your screen.
  • Without it, Outbound copies the prompt and you paste it.
  • Files are read only when you import them.

The clipboard

  • Using a prompt puts it on the clipboard.
  • Outbound reads the clipboard only when you paste into it.
  • Clipboard managers may save what Outbound copies.

Your control

  • Copy the file to back up or move your prompts.
  • Deleted prompts leave the file on the next save. ⌘Z restores them while Outbound is open.
  • To remove everything, quit Outbound, then delete the app and the Outbound folder in Application Support.

Changes

  • Any change will be posted here first.
  • Questions go to GitHub issues.
