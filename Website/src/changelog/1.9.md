### New

- Settings opens in its own window, like System Settings, with new Flight and Places sections.
- Fly straight, in Settings. The land comes straight down the screen, without drifting from side to side.
- Hide the seagull, or keep it from following the pointer.
- Choose which places come up, shuffle them, or put them in your own order.
- Choose how long it waits before holding still while you're away, or never.

### Changed

- The seagull menu is shorter and clearer. Speed is now Slow, Medium and Fast.
- The power settings moved from the menu to Settings, under Saving Power.
- New license keys work on one Mac at a time. Keys you already have still work on three.
- Settings, the buy window and the menu use the current macOS look.
