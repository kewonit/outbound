### New

- Opens on the view your desktop showed when you shut down.
- The menu says why the painting is paused. Fly To still works.
- Settings has buttons for Website, Support, Feedback and Check for Updates.
- Feedback sent from the app includes a line on how the painting runs on your Mac.

### Changed

- Pause When Hidden is on by default, for everyone. 1.5 had saved it as off without asking.
- It now pauses once windows cover 82% of every desktop. Terminal windows count.
- Settings are saved only when you change them, so off stays off.
- A hidden display draws 24 frames a second instead of 30. A 75 Hz display runs at 25 instead of 37.5.
- About 85% less GPU time spent painting the ground ahead.
- On Battery and Pause When Hidden have their own group in the menu.

### Fixed

- The wallpaper could freeze on slower Macs after 1.5. The landscape is now painted in small pieces, which should stop it.
- With Low Power Mode on battery, the lock screen could show your own picture or an old painting.
