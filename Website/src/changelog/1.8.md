### New

- Two places now meet under a thin band of cloud, instead of one turning into the other.
- Rivers, lakes and paddies are painted as water, and the surface moves.
- Rivers bend, with sandbars inside the bends and trees along the banks.
- New boats, with someone at the helm.
- Every home has a path to the road. Roads cross water on bridges, and the railway crosses roads on one.
- Kansas sits on its survey grid, oaks stand in the Normandy hedges, and Lisse has a new glasshouse and windmill.
- Show Place Name, in the menu. Untick it to hide the name in the corner of the desktop.

### Changed

- Slow, Gentle and Brisk are each about a third slower.
- The shortest stay over a place is twenty minutes. Twelve is gone.
- About two thirds fewer houses.
- Cars, boats, trains and herds move as fast as they really do.
- Shadows start at the foot of each tree.
- About 15% less GPU time for each frame.

### Fixed

- Crossroads could jump to a new spot as the flight went on.
- The pools on Lapland's mires were broken into blocks.
