### New

- Everything moves at about half speed, traffic and clouds included.
- Everyone starts on Slow once. Gentle and Brisk are still under Pace.
- New seagull. It flies in curves instead of turning on the spot.
- When you shut down, restart or log out, the flight stops first, so the screen goes dark on the same picture as your desktop.

### Changed

- The shortest stay over a place is twelve minutes. Six is gone.
- Flocks come about half as often.

### Fixed

- On very tall displays, a third place could show up before the last handover finished.
- A seagull saved mid-flap by 1.6 held its wings half closed.
