Lost your key

  • It's in your customer portal. Sign in with the email you paid with.
  • If you bought it in this browser, it's also on the homepage.

New Mac

  • One key works on 3 Macs at once.
  • To free one up, click Deactivate in Outbound's Settings, or use your customer portal.
  • On the new Mac, choose Unlock Outbound in the menu bar and paste your key.

Not moving

  • It pauses when the desktop is hidden, the screen sleeps or Low Power Mode is on.
  • It holds still after five minutes away, and wakes when you move the mouse.
  • Paused it yourself? Choose Resume in the menu bar.

Remove it

  • Choose Quit Outbound in the menu bar. Your own wallpaper comes back.
  • Then drag Outbound from Applications to the Trash.

Receipts

  • They're in your customer portal.
