Changelog.

What changed in each version of Outbound, newest first. Outbound updates itself, so there is nothing you need to download here.

On the Mac App Store, each version arrives once Apple has reviewed it. Every download is also on GitHub.

1.7

8 November 2026

Everything moves at about half the speed, the seagull is painted again, and the view comes to rest before your Mac shuts down.

New

  • Everything moves at about half the speed it did. Cars, boats, trains, flocks and clouds slow down with the view, so the scene plays as it did, only slower.
  • Everyone starts on Slow once, then keeps what they pick. Gentle and Brisk are still under Pace in the menu.
  • The seagull is painted again: real wings with dark tips, a body that tapers both ways, a short fan of a tail and a yellow bill, lit from the side the sun is on. It stays white.
  • It flies in slow curves and leans into its turns, instead of flipping round on the spot when the cursor passes behind it.
  • When you shut down, restart or log out, the flight slows to a stop first, so the screen goes dark on a still picture that matches your desktop. If you cancel, it flies on.

Changed

  • The shortest stay over a place is now twelve minutes. At the new pace, six minutes was too short for one place to hand over to the next.
  • Flocks come at their own rates again, about half as often as before.

Fixed

  • On very tall displays, a third place could come into view before the last one had finished turning into the next.
  • A seagull that 1.6 saved partway through a flap now finishes its wingbeat, instead of holding its wings half closed.

1.6

29 October 2026

Slower Macs paint in small steps, Pause When Hidden is on by default, and Outbound opens where your desktop left off.

New

  • Outbound opens on the same view your desktop showed when you shut down.
  • The menu says why the painting is holding still, and Fly To works while it does.
  • Settings has buttons for the website, support, feedback and Check for Updates.
  • Feedback sent from the app includes a short line about how the painting runs on your Mac.

Changed

  • Pause When Hidden is on by default. It holds the painting while windows cover nearly all of every desktop, and terminal windows count too.
  • It is on for everyone who had it off in 1.5, because 1.5 saved it as off whether you chose that or not. Each setting is now saved only when you change it, so if you turn it off, it stays off.
  • A display hidden behind windows while another still shows draws 24 frames a second instead of 30, and a 75 Hz display runs at 25 instead of 37.5.
  • Painting the ground ahead takes about 85% less GPU time, as it is painted in a few bigger steps instead of a row every frame.
  • On Battery and Pause When Hidden have a group of their own in the menu.

Fixed

  • Slower Macs now paint the landscape in small pieces, so a slow GPU never stops the wallpaper. This should fix the painting standing still on some Macs after 1.5.
  • On battery with Low Power Mode, desktops you switched to could keep your own picture or an older painting on the lock screen.

1.5

28 October 2026

Pause When Hidden, a way to send feedback from the menu, and the painted border off for everyone.

New

  • Pause When Hidden, in the menu, holds the flight while windows cover every desktop, and carries on from the same moment when the desktop shows again.
  • Feedback, in the menu, opens a page where you can write to me directly.

Changed

  • The painted border is off until you turn it on, for everyone, not only new installs.

1.4

27 October 2026

The painting on the lock screen, your own pictures back when you quit, and about half the memory.

New

  • The painting stays on the lock screen and the login window.
  • When you quit, your own picture comes back on every desktop, including the ones out of view.

Changed

  • Outbound uses about half the memory. On two displays it went from about 606 MB to 321 MB.
  • It starts sooner after an update.
  • New installs start without the painted border.

Fixed

  • Moving between desktops no longer makes the view jump.

1.3

27 October 2026

A new look for the land, fifteen more languages, and lights that stay steady.

New

  • Trees are painted together, with crowns that overlap and shadows that fall from the trunk. They keep off roads, houses and ponds.
  • Rivers bend, widen and narrow, with sand bars inside the bends.
  • Cars and boats keep to the road and the river round bends, and no longer swap places or vanish. Houses face the road, with their sheds behind.
  • Headlights are soft low beams on the road ahead, with small red tail lights, instead of hard white wedges.
  • Lapland, La Pampa and Niigata are drawn again.
  • Outbound speaks your Mac's language, in 15 more: Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Turkish, Polish, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Hindi and Arabic. Place names and seasons stay in English.
  • Time at Each Place has Do not change, which stays over one place until you pick another with Fly To.
  • The painted border can be turned off from the menu bar.

Changed

  • No light flickers any more. Street lamps are steady, a television lights its room with a steady glow, and the water no longer glints.
  • New copies start in daylight, with each place in its best season.

1.2

26 October 2026

Your choices move into the menu bar.

Changed

  • Light, seasons, pace, time at each place and battery choices are in the menu bar, under a new seagull icon.
  • Settings keeps the toggles and your license, and always opens in front of your other windows.
  • New copies start over Kansas, the place on the website.

1.1

26 October 2026

Smoother on every display, and it runs on macOS 13 Ventura and later.

New

  • Outbound runs on macOS 13 Ventura and later. 1.0 needed macOS 26.

Changed

  • Frames reach the screen evenly, 30 a second on power and 24 on battery, so a 60 Hz display shows 30 instead of 20.
  • The bird keeps up with the cursor, and Fly To no longer stutters.
  • On displays that aren't Retina, the ground no longer shimmers as it moves.

Fixed

  • Pasting a license key with ⌘V works, and stray spaces, line breaks and lowercase letters no longer get in the way.
  • Once Outbound is unlocked, the window says the bird in the menu bar is Outbound, and offers to open it at login.
  • The painting no longer stays behind on your other desktops after you quit.

1.0

26 October 2026

The first release. A painted landscape flies by behind your desktop over eight real places, the light follows your clock, and a little bird follows your cursor. Outbound keeps itself up to date from here on.
