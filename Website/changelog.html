Changelog.

1.8

12 November 2026

New

  • Two places now meet under a thin band of cloud, instead of one turning into the other.
  • Rivers, lakes and paddies are painted as water, and the surface moves.
  • Rivers bend, with sandbars inside the bends and trees along the banks.
  • New boats, with someone at the helm.
  • Every home has a path to the road. Roads cross water on bridges, and the railway crosses roads on one.
  • Kansas sits on its survey grid, oaks stand in the Normandy hedges, and Lisse has a new glasshouse and windmill.
  • Show Place Name, in the menu. Untick it to hide the name in the corner of the desktop.

Changed

  • Slow, Gentle and Brisk are each about a third slower.
  • The shortest stay over a place is twenty minutes. Twelve is gone.
  • About two thirds fewer houses.
  • Cars, boats, trains and herds move as fast as they really do.
  • Shadows start at the foot of each tree.
  • About 15% less GPU time for each frame.

Fixed

  • Crossroads could jump to a new spot as the flight went on.
  • The pools on Lapland's mires were broken into blocks.

1.7

8 November 2026

New

  • Everything moves at about half speed, traffic and clouds included.
  • Everyone starts on Slow once. Gentle and Brisk are still under Pace.
  • New seagull. It flies in curves instead of turning on the spot.
  • When you shut down, restart or log out, the flight stops first, so the screen goes dark on the same picture as your desktop.

Changed

  • The shortest stay over a place is twelve minutes. Six is gone.
  • Flocks come about half as often.

Fixed

  • On very tall displays, a third place could show up before the last handover finished.
  • A seagull saved mid-flap by 1.6 held its wings half closed.

1.6

29 October 2026

New

  • Opens on the view your desktop showed when you shut down.
  • The menu says why the painting is paused. Fly To still works.
  • Settings has buttons for Website, Support, Feedback and Check for Updates.
  • Feedback sent from the app includes a line on how the painting runs on your Mac.

Changed

  • Pause When Hidden is on by default, for everyone. 1.5 had saved it as off without asking.
  • It now pauses once windows cover 82% of every desktop. Terminal windows count.
  • Settings are saved only when you change them, so off stays off.
  • A hidden display draws 24 frames a second instead of 30. A 75 Hz display runs at 25 instead of 37.5.
  • About 85% less GPU time spent painting the ground ahead.
  • On Battery and Pause When Hidden have their own group in the menu.

Fixed

  • The wallpaper could freeze on slower Macs after 1.5. The landscape is now painted in small pieces, which should stop it.
  • With Low Power Mode on battery, the lock screen could show your own picture or an old painting.

1.5

28 October 2026

New

  • Pause When Hidden, in the menu. The flight pauses while windows cover every desktop.
  • Feedback, in the menu.

Changed

  • The painted border is off for everyone until you turn it on.

1.4

27 October 2026

New

  • The painting shows on the lock screen and the login window.
  • Quitting puts your own picture back on every desktop.

Changed

  • About half the memory: 606 MB down to 321 MB on two displays.
  • Starts faster after an update.
  • New installs start without the painted border.

Fixed

  • Switching desktops made the view jump.

1.3

27 October 2026

New

  • Trees with overlapping crowns and shadows, rivers that bend, and houses that face the road.
  • Cars and boats follow bends and no longer swap or vanish.
  • Headlights are soft low beams instead of white wedges.
  • Lapland, La Pampa and Niigata are redrawn.
  • 15 more languages: Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Turkish, Polish, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Hindi and Arabic.
  • Do not change, under Time at Each Place, stays over one place.
  • The painted border can be turned off from the menu.

Changed

  • No more flickering lights or glinting water.
  • New copies start in daylight, with each place in its best season.

1.2

26 October 2026

Changed

  • Light, seasons, pace, stay and battery options moved to the menu bar, under a new seagull icon.
  • Settings opens in front of other windows.
  • New copies start over Kansas.

1.1

26 October 2026

New

  • Runs on macOS 13 Ventura and later. 1.0 needed macOS 26.

Changed

  • Steady 30 frames a second, 24 on battery. A 60 Hz display gets 30 instead of 20.
  • The bird keeps up with the cursor, and Fly To no longer stutters.
  • No shimmer on displays that aren't Retina.

Fixed

  • ⌘V didn't paste a license key.
  • After quitting, the painting could stay on other desktops.
